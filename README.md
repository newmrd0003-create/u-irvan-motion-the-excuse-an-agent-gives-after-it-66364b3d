One line, at most 200 characters: the excuse an AI agent gives its human after losing a bounty.

`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`). Pure stdlib Python, deterministic.
